ABOUT

What is Moot Court AI?

Moot Court AI is an intelligent legal practice platform built for law students and advocates who want to sharpen their courtroom skills without waiting for scheduled moot court events.

Using advanced AI, the platform simulates real courtroom scenarios. You can argue as a Petitioner or Respondent, face cross-examination from an AI judge, cite legal precedents, and handle objections — all in real time.

After each session, receive a detailed performance evaluation with scores, strengths, weaknesses, and actionable tips to improve your advocacy skills.
